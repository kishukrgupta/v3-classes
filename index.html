About the Founder

Vikash Prakash

M.Sc. (Physics) — Founder & Director, V3 Classes

With a deep passion for Physics and a vision to transform education in Bihar, Vikash Prakash founded V3 Classes on 13th March 2016. Starting with just a handful of students and an unwavering belief that every student deserves access to quality coaching, he built V3 Classes into one of Khagaul's most trusted coaching institutes.

V3 stands for "Value of Your Vision and Visibility" — a philosophy that drives every classroom, every lesson, and every student interaction. Today, V3 Classes is home to over 5,000 successful alumni who have gone on to crack JEE, NEET, and board examinations with distinction.

V3 Classes offers comprehensive coaching for Classes IX, X, XI, XII (CBSE & Bihar Board), as well as specialized preparation for JEE (Main & Advanced) and NEET UG entrance exams. All major science subjects — Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology — are covered.
V3 Classes was founded by Vikash Prakash, who holds an M.Sc. in Physics. He established the institute on 13th March 2016 with the vision of providing quality education to students in Khagaul and surrounding areas of Bihar.
V3 stands for "Value of Your Vision and Visibility" — a philosophy that encourages every student to discover their potential, develop a clear vision for their future, and achieve visibility through academic excellence.
V3 Classes is located on the 2nd Floor, Beside Indian Overseas Bank, Near JNL College Road, Khagaul, Bihar. The institute is easily accessible and well-connected by public transport.
We maintain small batch sizes to ensure each student receives personalized attention. This allows our faculty to focus on individual learning needs and track the progress of every student closely.

Regular assessments are conducted weekly, including chapter-wise tests, unit tests, and full-length mock exams. Performance reports are shared with parents periodically to keep them updated on their child's progress.
